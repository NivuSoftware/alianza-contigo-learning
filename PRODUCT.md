# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Adult students in Ecuador seeking professional continuing education and institutionally endorsed certificates; teachers who review assessments and accompany learning; and administrators who manage the catalog, enrollments, content, evaluations, students, and certificates.

## Product Purpose

Alianza Contigo provides a trustworthy public course catalog and a coherent LMS workflow from enrollment through sequential learning, assessment, review, and certification. This repository is a functional frontend prototype with realistic mock data and clear seams for a future API and payment integrations.

## Brand Personality

Professional, educational, and confident. The experience should feel premium without becoming ornamental, and supportive without becoming informal or childish.

## Anti-references

Avoid childish gamification, saturated multi-color dashboards, generic SaaS glassmorphism, excessive gold, decorative motion, and dense academic portals that obscure the next action.

## Design Principles

1. Make the next learning or administrative action immediately clear.
2. Use institutional endorsement and progress evidence to earn trust.
3. Keep public discovery spacious while authenticated workflows remain efficient.
4. Reuse one consistent interaction vocabulary across all roles.
5. Demonstrate complete workflows with honest prototype data and no invented real prices.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.1 AA contrast and keyboard behavior. Respect reduced-motion preferences, never rely on color alone for learning states, use clear Spanish labels, and keep layouts usable on mobile through desktop.
